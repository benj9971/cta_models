# Public positioning and research price proxies

## LME commitments of traders

`dashboard/data/lme_{cu,al,zn,ni}_cotr_all_categories.csv` contains the public weekly workbooks linked on the LME copper, aluminium, zinc and nickel COT pages, as fetched 9 October 2026. Coverage is 3 January 2020 through 2 October 2026: 349 copper, 350 aluminium, 351 zinc and 352 nickel distinct position dates. There were 1,403 workbook links and 1,402 distinct reports after retaining the latest published copy of a duplicate date.

These CSVs retain **all five published trader categories**, each risk-reducing, other and total bucket, long and short lots, net lots, week-on-week changes, percent of open interest, trader counts where provided, position date, publication timestamp and source workbook URL. One row is a market/date/category/bucket. Zero positions remain zero. The parser verifies that risk-reducing plus other lots equals total lots. This is exchange-reported category positioning, not the CTA model and not a census of CTA holdings. The investment-funds category is only one of five categories.

To update: `python scripts/fetch_lme_cotr.py --output-dir dashboard/data` (requires `requests`, `openpyxl`, `xlrd`). The script refuses to save a partial archive when any linked workbook fails. The LME pages currently link back to January 2020; this dataset makes no claim of complete pre-2020 history. Check LME terms before redistributing or using these reports outside permitted research.

To chart the closest LME counterpart to managed money, run `python scripts/chart_lme_investment_funds.py --output-dir output/lme_funds`. Add `--refresh` to pull the public LME workbooks first, or `--market LME_CU` for just copper. The script exports a weekly joined CSV and full-history and three-year charts for each metal. It selects the published **investment funds / total** long and short positions and plots their net in lots above the observed Westmetall three-month quote on or before each position date. Its CSV retains the report publication timestamp; the Friday position was generally unknown until Tuesday. It does not equate LME investment funds to CFTC managed money or to CTA holdings. Nickel has two position weeks without a nearby quote during the 2022 trading disruption; these prices remain missing rather than being filled across the gap.

Source: Yahoo Finance chart API. Run `python scripts/fetch_research_price_proxies.py --output-dir dashboard/data --as-of YYYY-MM-DD` to refresh, or pass `--markets COMEX_GC COMEX_SI` to refresh only the new metals. The platinum vendor history before 2010 has long gaps and is omitted from the model-ready file. The files contain one daily close and an identical `signal_close` alias, with market, contract and source labels; no intraday range is inferred. The vendor's contract month mapping, roll selection, adjustments and timestamp conventions are unverified. Silver has an exceptionally large observed daily close move around 30 January 2026 (about −31% in this series); check that interval against contract-level settlements before interpreting its signal or risk. `TIO=F` is **not SGX FEF**; its basis, index reference and contract conventions may differ. These are research signals only. Do not treat percentage returns on these series as executable futures performance. The dashboard blocks backtests for bundled proxies.

From the repository root, run `python scripts/run_proxy_models.py --data-dir dashboard/data --output-dir <your-output-folder>` for position CSVs and full-history/three-year charts; `--markets COMEX_GC COMEX_SI` selects just gold and silver. The model uses 20/60/120/252 session horizons with 10/15/35/40% weights and a volatility risk scale. A score of +100 or −100 represents its modeled risk limit, **not actual CTA contracts or exchange-observed holdings**. On 8 October 2026 the provisional scores are COMEX gold −29.5, silver −18.1, copper +25.9, NYMEX platinum −22.0 and CME iron ore proxy for SGX −67.5. The model takes signals from close alone; RSI(14) can likewise be computed from closes without high/low or intraday bars.

CFTC disaggregated futures-only history for gold and silver is in `cftc_disaggregated_futures_only_gc_si.csv` (1,060 Tuesday dates per metal from 13 June 2006 through 29 September 2026). Refresh it with `python -m cta_models.cot --output dashboard/data/cftc_disaggregated_futures_only_gc_si.csv --markets COMEX_GC COMEX_SI`. This reports managed money, not CTA-only positions, and is released after its position date.
